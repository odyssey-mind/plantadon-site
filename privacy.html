Plantadon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Plantadon ("we", "our", "us") is a vegan ingredient scanner app for Android, with a website at plantadon.app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect in the App

  • Account information — if you create an account, we collect your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account name and email.
  • Scan history — barcodes you scan, the verdict returned, your scan settings, and the timestamp of each scan. This is stored on our servers and linked to your account if you are signed in, or to your device identifier if you are not.
  • Ingredient label scans — if you scan an ingredient label, the text is recognised on your device and only that text (not the photo) is sent to our servers. We store the text and the ingredients parsed from it with the product's barcode, so that other people who scan the same product can get a verdict. Label text is product information, not personal information.
  • Subscription status — whether you hold an active Plantadon Pro subscription, managed via RevenueCat.
  • Device identifier (anonymous use) — if you use Plantadon without an account, your device generates a random identifier that is stored on your device and sent with your scan requests. This lets us show you your own recent scans without an account and keeps your scans separate from other users'. It is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites. If you later create an account, your anonymous scan history is not carried over.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide scan verdicts and ingredient analysis
  • To display your scan history within the app
  • To improve product coverage for all users (label scans)
  • To verify your Pro subscription status
  • To authenticate your account securely and send account emails (such as verification codes)

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not serve ads and do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not sell your data to any third party
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not access your photos or camera roll, and we do not upload camera images
  • The anonymous device identifier is not used for cross-app tracking, advertising, or profiling

4. Third-Party Services

  • Amazon Web Services — hosts our servers and database, handles account authentication (Cognito), and sends account emails (SES). AWS Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — handles subscription management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google — optional sign-in with Google, and Google Play for app distribution and purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — hosts our website, provides website analytics, and forwards email sent to our plantadon.app addresses. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Open Food Facts — our product and ingredient database source. This data is publicly available and contains no personal information. Open Food Facts Privacy Policy

5. Our Website (plantadon.app)

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It is designed not to use cookies, local storage, or device fingerprinting, and it does not track you across other websites. It records aggregate information such as the page visited, the referring website, browser and operating system type, and country. We use it only to understand how many people visit and how they found us.

Links from our website to the Google Play Store may include a referral tag naming the channel you came from (for example, "instagram"). The tag contains no personal information.

6. Data Retention

Account data and scan history are retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and its associated data at any time (see Your Rights).

Anonymous scan history is stored on our servers against your device identifier. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app creates a new identifier, after which earlier anonymous history is no longer accessible from the app; it remains on our servers but is not linked to you by name, email, or any other personal information.

Ingredient label text is retained as product information. We record which account or device submitted each label scan, to maintain data quality; other users see only the product information, never who submitted it.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Alternatively, you can request deletion of your account through our account deletion page. When we delete your account, we delete your email address, sign-in record, subscription record, and Allowed Ingredients list. Your scan history and any ingredient label scans you submitted are kept only in anonymized form: they are permanently disconnected from your account and from any information that identifies you, and are used to improve product data for all users. If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it in Google Play first; deleting your account does not cancel billing.

8. Children

Plantadon is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app or website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
