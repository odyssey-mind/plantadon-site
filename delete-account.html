Plantadon — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 2026

You may request deletion of your Plantadon account at any time. Plantadon can be used without an account; this page applies only if you created one.

Before you start: cancel any Pro subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel billing. If you have Plantadon Pro, cancel it first in the Google Play Store: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Plantadon → Cancel subscription.

How to request deletion

Send an email to support@plantadon.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Plantadon account.

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email once complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email address and, if used, your Google sign-in link)
  • Your subscription records
  • Your Allowed Ingredients list and saved preferences

What is retained

Your scan history (barcodes, verdicts and timestamps) and any ingredient label text you scanned may be retained in permanently anonymised form — with all links to your account, email address, and any personally identifiable information irreversibly removed — for the purpose of improving Plantadon's product and ingredient data. This anonymised data cannot be traced back to you in any way.

Questions

See our Privacy Policy or contact support@plantadon.app.
