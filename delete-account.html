Plantadon — Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: May 2026

You may request deletion of your Plantadon account and all associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

Send an email to odysseymind01@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Plantadon account.

We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email once complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials
  • Your full scan history
  • Your dietary preferences and ignore list
  • Your subscription records

What is retained

No personally identifiable data is retained after deletion. Anonymised aggregate usage data (with no link to your account) may be retained for product improvement purposes.
